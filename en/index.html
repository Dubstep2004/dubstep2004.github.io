    FAQ

    Is APEX VPN the same as APEX CLIENT?

    Yes. Since version 3.19.1 the app is called APEX CLIENT — only the name changed. It updates in place, your subscription and settings stay.

    Is it free?

    The app is free and open source (GPL-3.0). To connect you need a subscription — a link to your servers.

    Where do I get a subscription?

    The service you connect to gives it to you. Paste the link, scan a QR code or open an invite link.

    Is it safe to install outside Google Play?

    Every version is built on GitHub from open source code and signed with one key. The app verifies it’s the original and warns you if a copy was tampered with.

    Sites don’t open under whitelists

    Choose Autopilot — it finds the LTE bypass by itself. If you pick the bypass manually, all traffic goes through the VPN so Russian sites open too.

    Something doesn’t work — who do I contact?

    In the app: Settings → About → “Contact support”, or @Apexx_support on Telegram. You can send a report in one tap — without your subscription link, keys or IP.
